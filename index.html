03 · Past simple: the rules

Regular verbs

play → played
train → trained
watch → watched
stop → stopped
try → tried

Irregular verbs

go → went
run → ran
win → won
lose → lost
see → saw
have → had
do → did
swim → swam

Questions & negatives

Did you play?
Yes, I did.
I didn't play.
She didn't win.

Important: After did/didn't, always use the base form. “Did you go?” ✓ “Did you went?” ✗

08 · Reading: the unforgettable match

Last Saturday, Maya and her friends played in a school basketball tournament. They trained hard all week. Their first match started at ten. Maya scored eight points, but her team lost the first game. In the afternoon, they played again. This time, they ran faster and worked together. Maya made a great pass and Leo scored the winning basket. They won the final 24–22. After the match, they went for pizza and took a team photo. Everyone felt proud.
